Get the full version

This website is the library. The full version is a small, free add-on for Chrome on a laptop: it shows the script of any YouTube video automatically, line by line, with shadowing, slow replay and voice recording.

Download the full version (free)

  1. Download the file above and unzip it (double-click it).
  2. Open Chrome on the laptop and type chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Done! Open any YouTube video with subtitles: the script appears next to it.

Works with Chrome on a laptop or computer. Completely free, no account needed.
